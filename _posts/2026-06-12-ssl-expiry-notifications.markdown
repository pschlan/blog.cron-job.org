You can now ask cron-job.org to send you a notification when the SSL/TLS certificate of your website is about to expire. You can enable this notification per job, with a configurable notice period.

![Screenshot of the new setting in the job editor](/assets/2026/06/ssl-expiry-notification.png)

As usual, if you have any feedback or suggestions for us, please let us know at [info@cron-job.org](mailto:info@cron-job.org).
