You can now add ongoing and past incidents to your status pages. In the cron-job.org console, edit your status page and you will find the incident manager below the "Common" section.

![Screenshot of the new incidents manager](/assets/2026/06/incidents-manager.png)

Ongoing incidents will be displayed at the top of your status page,...

![Screenshot of exemplary ongoing incidents on a demo status page](/assets/2026/06/ongoing-incidents.png)

...while a history of the past closed incidents of the last 30 days will be shown at the very bottom.

![Screenshot of exemplary past incidents on a demo status page](/assets/2026/06/past-incidents.png)

As usual, if you have any feedback or suggestions for us, please let us know at [info@cron-job.org](mailto:info@cron-job.org).
